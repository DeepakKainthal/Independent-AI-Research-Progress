# Questions I Am Still Investigating

I am keeping this list focused on **measurable research problems**, not internal implementation choices.

1. **Whole-task correctness:** How should a multi-step system be tested when a high percentage of correct individual steps can still leave many full tasks incorrect?
2. **Learning reliability:** Which independent evaluation designs best identify harmful knowledge updates and regressions in previously learned material?
3. **Evaluation validity:** What stress tests would best distinguish gains on generated tasks from improvements that also transfer to real-world, held-out tasks?
4. **Efficiency measurements:** Which hardware-level measurements are necessary before claiming improvements in energy, memory, or latency?

Constructive suggestions about **testing and evaluation** are welcome. Detailed implementation methods are not part of this public record.
